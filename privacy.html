Gantt MVP Privacy Policy

Publisher: AS Simple Apps
Policy date: 12 October 2026
Contact: sarjai.91@gmail.com

Purpose and information used

Gantt MVP provides configurable Jira timelines. It reads project configuration, issue types, statuses, selected date and user fields, ticket identifiers and the ticket fields requested for inline details. Depending on the selected fields, this can include account IDs, display names and information entered in Jira tickets. Access is subject to the current user's Jira permissions.

Information stored

The app stores project setup and each user's display preferences, including day/week granularity and inline-detail field choices, in Atlassian Forge hosted storage. The preference records are associated with an Atlassian account ID, project and issue type. Jira tickets and user profiles are retrieved when needed and are not copied into persistent app storage.

Hosting and sharing

The production app uses Atlassian Forge compute and storage. It has no external database, advertising service or third-party analytics integration, and no configured external data egress. Atlassian processes platform data under its own policies and Privacy Policy.

Information you voluntarily send to the support email is processed to respond to your request through the publisher's email service. Please do not send passwords, API tokens or unnecessary ticket contents.

Read-only Jira access

The app does not create, edit, delete, assign or transition Jira tickets. Saving app setup or display preferences changes app settings only. Shared project setup is restricted to project or Jira administrators.

Retention and privacy requests

Project settings and saved preferences are retained to provide the app's configuration and display behavior until changed or deleted. Atlassian currently retains Forge hosted storage for 28 days after an app is uninstalled, under its storage lifecycle.

Contact the publisher at the email above for questions about stored app preferences or to request access, correction or deletion. Requests about source Jira tickets or organization-managed accounts should also be directed to the relevant Jira administrator.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. The policy date identifies the version being presented.
